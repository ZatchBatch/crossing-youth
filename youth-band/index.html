Getting the music

Get into SongSelect.

  1. Create a free account at songselect.ccli.com.
  2. In your profile, join our organization with this code:
    4080094-01-7967
  3. Tap a song above to go straight to it, or search by title, artist, or song number.
  4. Below the album art, explore the tabs:
    GeneralOverview and song details
    LyricsWords only, formatted how you like
    ChordsSheet music with transpose and formatting tools
    LeadThe melody on a staff, for music readers
  5. Look for the Rehearse icon in the top banner. It opens a mixable audio track to play along with. The song links above open the recording we're using, so just hit play.
Using the Rehearse tool

Make it fit our band.

Our instrumentation doesn't match the album, so use the recording to get inspired and make up a part that fits. You can adjust:

KEY

Key

Make sure it matches our key!

BPM

Tempo

Slow it down if a part is tricky.

MIX

Mix preset & mix

Hit Up to feature an instrument, Down to mute it, or Mixer for a fully custom blend.
